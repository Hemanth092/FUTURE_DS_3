# FUTURE_DS_03 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign data to understand customer conversion performance, contact channel effectiveness, previous campaign outcomes, and monthly conversion patterns.

The project was completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 3.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall marketing conversion performance
- Identify conversion patterns across contact channels
- Analyze previous campaign outcomes
- Study monthly conversion trends
- Identify opportunities to improve lead-to-customer conversion

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- CSV Dataset
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

- Total Customers/Leads: 41,188
- Converted Customers: 4,640
- Overall Conversion Rate: 11.27%

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes:

- Overall Conversion
- Marketing Funnel
- Conversions by Contact Channel
- Conversion Rate by Contact Channel
- Conversions by Month
- Previous Campaign Outcome
- Interactive filters for Month, Job, and Contact

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 41,188 customer/lead records were analyzed.
- 4,640 customers converted, resulting in an overall conversion rate of 11.27%.
- Cellular contact showed a higher conversion rate than telephone contact.
- Previous campaign success was associated with a substantially higher conversion rate than previous failure or no previous campaign outcome.
- Conversion rates varied across different months.
- Customer job categories showed differences in conversion patterns.

## 💡 Recommendations

- Analyze why cellular campaigns show stronger conversion performance.
- Use previous campaign outcomes to support targeted follow-up strategies.
- Investigate the characteristics of high-conversion months.
- Segment campaigns based on customer characteristics instead of using a single approach for all leads.
